> Get started developing for Sony PlayStation 4 with Unreal Engine.

Get started developing for Playstation 4 with Unreal Engine.

Access to the Unreal Engine Playstation 4 documentation is available without additional cost or licensing to any Unreal Engine developer who has met all NDA requirements and been granted the necessary permissions.

To gain access to Playstation 4 documentation:

- If your company has already completed the console request process, ask your administrator to grant you Playstation 4 access from the Unreal Engine > Consoles page on the Epic Developer Portal.

- If your company or you have not already begun the console request process then submit a **first time** or **additional access** request at forms.unrealengine.com/s/form-console-access-request. - You will need to provide confirmation of you or your company’s approved Playstation 4 developer status and may need to accept an Epic Games Console NDA. Epic staff will guide you through the process.

> **info:** Console documentation is not currently provided on the UE documentation site. Instead it is available for download from a thread on the private Playstation 4 Development forum for Unreal Engine.
