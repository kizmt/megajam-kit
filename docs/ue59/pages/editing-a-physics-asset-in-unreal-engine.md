# Editing a Physics Asset

https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/editing-a-physics-asset-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.9

> This tutorial covers the procedure for opening an Physics Asset for editing in the Physics Asset Editor.

[image: Editing a Physics Asset]

Below are the steps for opening a **Physics Asset** for editing in the **Physics Asset Editor**.

## Steps

- Find the **Physics Asset** in the **Content Drawer**. [image: Find the Physics Asset in the Content Drawer]

- Double-click it to open it in the Physics Asset Editor. [image: Double-click it to open it in the Physics Asset Editor]

## Result

The Physics Asset will be open and ready to edit in the Physics Asset Editor.
