# Supported Video Devices for Mixed Reality Capture

https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/supported-video-devices-for-mixed-reality-capture-in-unreal-engine?application_version=5.9

> Supported video devices for use with the Mixed Reality Capture feature.

[image: Supported Video Devices for Mixed Reality Capture]

Learn to use this **Beta** feature, but use caution when shipping with it.

We have tested a limited number of video capture devices with the Mixed Reality Capture Framework (MRC). This is not to say that other video capture devices won't work, but below is a list of the devices that we've tested here at Epic Games.

**Webcams**

- Logitech C920

- Logitech C930e

**USB Capture Dongles**

- Magewell USB Capture HDMI Gen 2¹

¹ While untested, it is likely that other Magewell USB capture products are just as compatible with MRC, such as their Plus model (for 4K), and their SDI capture dongles (for broadcast hardware).

It is possible for advanced users to write a Media Framework plugin to support their own video capture device.
